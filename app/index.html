Seat Wheel

Full screen is blocked here. Press F11 (Windows) or Control + Command + F (Mac) instead.

Seat assignments

    Absent today

    Uncheck anyone who is out. They are taken off the wheel until you check them again.

    Settings

    Classes

    Up to 6 classes. Paste one name per line. Everything is saved on this computer automatically, so you only do this once.

    Look

    Use your school colors. Color 1 is the buttons, Color 2 is the pointer, and all three fill the wheel.

    Sounds

    One checked sound plays at random when the wheel stops, never the same one twice in a row.

    Built-in sounds

    Your sounds

    None yet. Add MP3, WAV, M4A or OGG files (short clips work best, up to 10 MB each).

    Backup and share

    Save a backup file to move your classes, colors and sounds to another computer or share the setup with another teacher. Opening a backup replaces what is here now.
